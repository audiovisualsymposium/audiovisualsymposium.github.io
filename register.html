Symposium Registration

Join us on 26–27 November 2026 at Linköping University Campus Norrköping for two days of research, artistic practice, discussion, and exploration across audiovisual studies.

Registration for the Audiovisual Symposium 2026 is free. Afternoon coffee or tea on Thursday, 26 November, as well as mid-morning coffee or tea on Friday, 27 November, are included in the conference registration and will be served at the Visualization Center.

Lunch Friday 27 November

Lunch on Friday 27 November is included for symposium attendees with accepted contributions and will be served at Norrköping Visualization Center C (see Symposium Venue). For symposium attendees without accepted contributions, lunch must be arranged independently.

  • Norrköping Visualization Center C has lunch for 119 SEK.

Dinner Thursday 26 November

Dinner will be organized at the Evening venue, and is not included in the registration.

Please complete the registration form to attend the symposium. We look forward to welcoming you to Norrköping!
