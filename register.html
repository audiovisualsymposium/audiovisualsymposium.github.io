Symposium Registration

Join us on 26–27 November 2026 at Linköping University Campus Norrköping for two days of research, artistic practice, discussion, and exploration across audiovisual studies.

Registration for the Audiovisual Symposium 2026 is free. Afternoon coffee or tea on Thursday, 26 November, as well as mid-morning coffee or tea on Friday, 27 November, are included in the conference registration and will be served at the Norrköping Visualization Center C (see Symposium Venue).

Lunch Friday 27 November

Lunch on Friday 27 November is included for symposium attendees with accepted contributions and will be served at Visualization Center. For symposium attendees without accepted contributions, lunch must be arranged independently.

  • Norrköping Visualization Center C serves lunch for 119 SEK.
  • Paradis du Liban serves lunch for 100-200 SEK, and is a 4-minute-walk from the symposium venue.
  • Tokio serves lunch for 100-200 SEK, and is a 3-minute-walk from the symposium venue.
  • Mocca Deli serves lunch for 100-200 SEK, and is a 5-minute-walk from the symposium venue.
  • Cafe Mjönaren serves lunch for 100-200 SEK, and is a 5-minute-walk from the symposium venue.
  • Mannam serves lunch for 100-200 SEK, and is a 6-minute-walk from the symposium venue.

Dinner Thursday 26 November

Dinner will be held at the Evening venue, and is not included in the registration.

If you have submitted a contribution to the symposium, please wait with registration until after you have received the letter of acceptance (9th of October 2026).

Please complete the registration form to attend the symposium.
