The City of Norrköping

The symposium will be held in the beautifully preserved industrial heart of the city of Norrköping. The city is located approximately 150 kilometers south of Stockholm. Trains are running regularly from Arlanda airport just north of Stockholm. Trains are either running from Arlanda to Stockholm and then another train from Stockholm to Norrköping, or direct trains from Arlanda to Norrköping. Stockholm Arlanda airport has many connections over the world.

The airport in the city of Linköping, 45 kilometers southwest of Norrköping, has flight connections to Schiphol airport in the Netherlands, and there are trains running from Linköping to Norrköping approximately every 20 minutes.

It is also possible to travel to Copenhagen in Denmark and take the train from Copenhagen airport towards Stockholm.
