Audiovisual Symposium Notes 2022

1st Audiovisual Symposium 2022, 25 November, Falun, Sweden.

The DAVAMOT Audiovisual Symposium is part of DAVAMOT (24th – 26th November), a festival for everything audiovisual, it is a filmfestival, exhibition for audiovisual art and audiovisual installations, a music festival, and a symposium on the audiovisual phenomenon, all the above combined into a meeting, or mot in Old Norse. DAVAMOT celebrates the anniversary of the Media House in Falun. For 20 years, the Media House has been the place for a unique academic environment that combines audiovisual research, education and art. With a sound studio, TV studio, radio studio, sound lab, editing room, and our own cinema, the Media House is an audiovisual laboratory for new knowledge and new expressions.

The symposium Notes published here have undergone a double review process, first a peer review prior to the symposium and then a further review and revision of the contributions.
